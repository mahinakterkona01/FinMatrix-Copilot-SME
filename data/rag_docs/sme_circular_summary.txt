SMESPD Circular Letter No. 04 (2026): CMSME Working Capital Refinance Fund.
Eligibility: Small and Medium Enterprises operating in manufacturing, service, and trading sectors.
Interest Rate: Refinance facility provided at capped interest rate of 7% to 8% for end borrowers.
Collateral Policy: Special credit guarantee scheme available for cottage and micro enterprises without traditional collateral.

SMESPD Circular Letter No. 03 (2026): JICA assisted Urban Building Safety Project (UBSP, BD-P84).
Purpose: Financial support for retrofitting, rebuilding, and safety enhancement for industrial and SME infrastructure in urban areas.

SMESPD Circular Letter No. 05 (2026): Credit Guarantee Scheme (CGS) & Refinance Guidelines for CMSME.
Facility: Collateral-free loan facility coverage under Bangladesh Bank Credit Guarantee Scheme for Micro, Cottage, and Small Enterprises (CMSE).
Margin & Fee: Capped guarantee fee and removal of hidden processing fees to ease SME loan access.
